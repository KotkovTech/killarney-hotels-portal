THE BROOK LODGE EXPERIENCE

Your Private Oasis Off High Street

This is a classic old-school weekend retreat, the sort of place to come for proper peace and quiet, to get away from the noise of people needing to see and be seen. Instead everyone is here for the great variety of a deep night’s sleep and the chance to sign off from day-to-day stress.

The deeply pretty Georgian manor house sits surrounded by exquisite gardens, stone fountains, and an avenue of sky-high beech and lime trees. With 30 Guest rooms and Suites, the hotel offers a true quintessentially Irish understated luxury experience.
